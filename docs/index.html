Find dead or deactivated accounts optional

Instagram only answers "does this account still exist?" for a logged-in session — every profile URL returns the same generic page when you are logged out. So this step runs in your own Instagram tab, with your session, and sends the results straight back here. Nothing is uploaded; the site never sees your password or cookies.

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar (desktop):
    loading…
  2. Open instagram.com in that same browser and make sure you're logged in.
  3. Click the bookmark. A panel appears and checks every account one by one (~1 per second).
  4. When it finishes, it copies the results and reopens this page with them filled in.

How it works

Instagram lets you download everything it knows about your account, including followers_1.json and following.json. This page parses those two lists directly and compares them. No scraping, no automation, no password, and no request leaves your device.

ListMeaning
Followingaccounts you follow
Followersaccounts that follow you
Doesn't follow backyou follow, they don't
Follows you onlythey follow, you don't
Mutualboth

Counts describe the moment Instagram captured the export — that can be a day or two old, because big accounts take a while to generate.
